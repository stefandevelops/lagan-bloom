    About us

    A corner shop that happens to sell flowers.

    Niamh opened Lagan & Bloom in 2017 after ten years working for florists in London and Dublin. She wanted a shop that felt like a local: somewhere you could pop in for a fiver's worth of tulips as easily as order your wedding flowers.

    Today there are six of us. From May to October around half our flowers are grown here in Northern Ireland, by small growers in Down, Armagh and Antrim. The rest come from the Dutch auctions via a wholesaler in Belfast. We compost all our green waste and our cargo bike does most of the city-centre runs.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Can't find the answer? Ring the shop on 028 9649 6123 and you'll get a florist, not a call centre.

    Can I choose a delivery time?

    We deliver between 10am and 6pm. We can't promise exact times, but add a note at checkout (for example, “before 1pm, office closes”) and we'll route around it. For funerals we always deliver to the funeral director's timetable.

    What if nobody's home?

    We'll try a neighbour first, then leave the flowers somewhere safe and shaded, and text you a photo. If neither works, we'll bring them back and call you.

    Do the flowers look exactly like the photos?

    The colours, size and style will match. The exact stems change week to week with what's good at the market, which is why our flowers last longer than most.

    How long will they last?

    Most of our bouquets last 7 to 10 days. Trim the stems, change the water every couple of days and keep them away from the fruit bowl and radiators.

    Do you deliver to hospitals?

    The Royal, the City and the Ulster don't accept flowers on wards. We're happy to deliver to the main reception for a patient's family to collect, or straight to their home for when they're back.

    Can I order for Mother's Day or Valentine's Day?

    Yes. Pre-orders open about four weeks before each date. Same-day cut-off is earlier on those days, usually 11am.
